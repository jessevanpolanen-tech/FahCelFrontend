How it works
01

It starts the moment a reading lands.

A wireless logger reports temperature, humidity, shock and light. FahCel seals each reading into a hash chain the instant it arrives — before anyone could touch it.

02

The seal rides with the pallet.

Across every handoff — dock, truck, port, store — the chain travels with the goods, logging location and door-open events the whole way.

03

Excursions surface in real time.

Threshold breaches hit the right team immediately, and the alerting record is already sealed — not reconstructed after the fact.

04

Proof, ready before the auditor asks.

Hand inspectors a signed history with a one-click integrity check. The cryptography did the work; you just get the readiness.

Tamper-evident by design

Every record carries the DNA of the one before it.

  • Linked records. Each reading hashes the one before it, so altering history breaks the chain.
  • Deterministic. Anyone can recompute the proof and get the same verdict — offline.
  • One-click verify. Walk the whole chain and pinpoint the first reading that was touched.
